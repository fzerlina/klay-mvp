# Manual Journal Entry — competitor screenshots

Collected 25 Oct 2026 for the Klay manual JE page. Images are each vendor's own
help-centre or blog screenshots (© the vendor), kept for internal reference
only. Credit the vendor and link the source article when reusing them.

No public JE screenshots found for DualEntry docs, Light, NetSuite, Sage
Intacct, Xero, QuickBooks Online or Odoo: their help pages are text-only,
JS-rendered, or behind a login. DualEntry's one image below is from its
marketing site.
